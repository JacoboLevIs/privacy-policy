2. Description of Service

Baby Tracker IL is a mobile application that helps parents and caregivers track pregnancy progress and baby development. Features include:

  • Pregnancy week-by-week tracking and contraction timer.
  • Newborn tracking: feeding, sleep, and diaper logs.
  • Growth charts based on WHO standards.
  • Developmental milestone tracking.
  • Vaccination and medical checkup schedules (based on Israeli Ministry of Health guidelines).
  • Tooth eruption map.
  • AI-powered parenting assistant ("Is This Normal?").
  • Multi-caregiver profile sharing.
  • Push notification reminders for feeding, sleep, vaccines, checkups, and milestones.

Features may vary between the free and premium plans, and may be modified, added, or removed at any time with reasonable notice.
